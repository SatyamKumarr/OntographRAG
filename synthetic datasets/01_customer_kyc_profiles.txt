1. Purpose

This document describes the Know Your Customer (KYC) profiles maintained by Meridian National Bank.

KYC is a customer due diligence process used to establish customer identity, understand the nature of the banking relationship, evaluate customer risk, and maintain compliance with applicable regulatory obligations.

KYC information is used during:

New customer onboarding.
Account opening.
Financial product enrollment.
Periodic customer reviews.
Enhanced due diligence.
AML transaction investigations.
Regulatory compliance assessments.

A KYC profile is represented by the KYC ontology class.

Each profile is linked operationally to one Customer and may be referenced by multiple ComplianceAssessment records.

7. KYC Lifecycle Management

KYC operations follow a controlled lifecycle:

Customer identification.
Identity-document collection.
Identity and address verification.
Customer due diligence.
Risk classification.
Sanctions and screening checks.
Compliance approval.
Periodic review.
Enhanced due diligence when necessary.
Profile update and evidence retention.

A KYC profile may remain verified while a separate enhanced due diligence review is pending.

Therefore, KYC verification status and customer risk classification must be treated as separate business concepts.

8. KYC and AML Integration
KYC data provides the baseline customer profile used by AML transaction monitoring.
When actual financial activity differs materially from expected activity, a monitoring alert may be generated.

An alert may lead to:
Transaction review.
Customer clarification.
Enhanced due diligence.
AML case creation.
Compliance escalation.
Suspicious Activity Report assessment.

Not every alert results in an AML case, and not every AML investigation results in a regulatory filing.
