CLEARING AND SETTLEMENT OPERATIONS
1. Clearing
Clearing is the process of exchanging transaction information and calculating obligations between participating financial institutions. Clearing records contain transaction references, participant information, amounts, processing dates, and clearing status.

2. Settlement
Settlement represents the completion of the financial obligation between relevant parties. A payment can be authorized without being settled. Similarly, a payment can be executed but require reconciliation before the operational record is considered fully closed.

5. Operational distinctions
Authorization confirms whether a payment can proceed.Clearing calculates and exchanges payment obligations.Settlement completes the financial obligation.Reconciliation verifies consistency between records.These processes must not be treated as interchangeable.
