1. Purpose and Scope

This document maintains the consolidated customer master records of Meridian National Bank.
The Customer entity represents an individual or organization that maintains a financial relationship with the institution.
Customer master data provides the foundation for account opening, KYC verification, transaction monitoring, financial product eligibility, regulatory reporting, customer service, and risk assessment.

Each customer is assigned a unique Customer ID. The identifier is used to connect customer information with accounts, financial products, KYC profiles, compliance assessments, transactions, customer complaints, and AML investigations. Customer master data must remain consistent across all banking systems.

7. Customer Master Data Governance
Customer master records are maintained by the Customer Relationship Management function.

The Customer entity is connected to multiple business domains:
Customer connects to Account through account ownership.
Customer connects to KYC through identity verification.
Customer connects to ComplianceAssessment through compliance review.
Customer connects to FinancialProduct through product enrollment.
Customer connects to FinancialTransaction through transaction participation.
Customer connects to AMLCase when suspicious activity requires investigation.
Customer connects to CustomerComplaint through service-related events.
Customer connects to Consent through privacy and communication preferences.
Customer connects to Employee through relationship management.
Customer connects to Branch through servicing responsibility.

These connections are operational business relationships, not additional customer attributes.

8. Data Quality and Control Requirements
Every customer must have a unique Customer ID.
Customer names must be maintained consistently across customer and account systems.
Each active customer must have an associated KYC profile.
Customer risk classifications must be supported by documented assessments.
Changes to customer information must be recorded with timestamps and responsible employees.
Customer records must not contain unmasked authentication credentials or payment-card security data.
Customer master records must be reviewed periodically.
