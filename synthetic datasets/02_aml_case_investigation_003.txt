2. Background
The merchant monitoring platform identified an unusual increase in transaction volume associated with merchant MER-3001.
The merchant primarily operated an online consumer electronics business.
Historical activity indicated relatively stable payment volumes and a normal proportion of refunds and chargebacks.

During the review period, the monitoring platform observed:

A sharp increase in transaction value.
A significant increase in refunds.
Multiple customer disputes.
Inconsistencies between payment and settlement records.
Unusual relationships between payment transactions and refund events.

The activity was referred to the Financial Crime Compliance Department.

5. Payment Processing Analysis

The payment processing team examined the relationship between the original payment records, authorization messages, clearing records, settlement instructions, and subsequent refunds.

The following systems were reviewed:

Payment processing platform.
ISO 8583 authorization message repository.
Merchant transaction ledger.
Clearing records.
Settlement records.
Refund processing platform.
Chargeback management system.

The purpose was to establish whether payment and refund records represented legitimate commercial activity and whether all transactions had been correctly reconciled.

6. Merchant Risk Indicators

The review identified several indicators requiring further investigation:

Unusual growth in transaction value.
Increased refund frequency.
Higher-than-normal customer dispute levels.
Discrepancies between payment and refund documentation.
Potentially inconsistent merchant transaction descriptions.
Incomplete supporting sales documentation.

These indicators were treated as investigative findings rather than proof of intentional misconduct.

8. Investigation Findings

The investigation established that the merchant's transaction profile changed materially during the review period.

Refund activity and customer disputes increased relative to historical levels.

The investigation team identified discrepancies requiring reconciliation between the merchant's payment records and the settlement platform.

The compliance team requested additional business documentation, including invoices, delivery confirmations, and refund justifications.

AML-2026-003 concerns unusual payment, refund, and chargeback behavior associated with merchant MER-3001. The investigation identified INR 830,000 in payment activity, INR 340,000 in refunds, and a disputed chargeback of INR 210,000.

The investigation requires cross-system verification of payment authorization, ISO 8583 messages, clearing, settlement, reconciliation, refund, and chargeback records. The case connects AML, payment processing, merchant management, financial risk, customer service, and compliance domains.
