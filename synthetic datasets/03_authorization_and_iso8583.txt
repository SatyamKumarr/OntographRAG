1. Authorization overview
Authorization is the process through which the payment processing platform determines whether a payment request can proceed.

Authorization considers:
Account validity.
Customer authentication.
Available balance or credit.
Transaction amount.
Transaction limits.
Merchant validity.
Card status.
Fraud indicators.
Compliance restrictions.

An Authorization entity represents the business authorization event.
