6. Root cause and recurring issue monitoring
Customer complaint analysis should identify recurring issues rather than treating every complaint as an isolated service event.

Important monitoring areas include:
Payment-related issues: Delays, disputed transactions, and refund processing problems.
Loan servicing issues: Incorrect installment calculations, repayment posting, and loan account discrepancies.
Digital banking issues: Login difficulties, service interruptions, and authentication problems.
Account servicing issues: Incorrect charges, account information updates, and communication delays.
Data management issues: Personal information correction requests and customer record inconsistencies.
Root cause findings should be used to identify process improvements and prevent recurrence.
