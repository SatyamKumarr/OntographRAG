1. Refund overview
A refund is a financial transaction that returns funds to a customer following an eligible original payment.

Refunds may result from:
Product returns.
Service cancellation.
Duplicate payment.
Merchant adjustment.
Failed delivery.
Approved customer dispute.

A refund should retain a reference to the original payment.

4. Important distinction

A refund is not the same as a reversal.A reversal generally cancels or compensates a payment that did not complete correctly. A refund generally returns funds after a completed payment.
