PAYMENT RECONCILIATION REPORTS
Reporting Period: October 2026

1. Reconciliation overview
Reconciliation compares transaction records across internal banking systems and external payment networks.

The objective is to identify:
Missing transactions.
Duplicate entries.
Amount mismatches.
Settlement delays.
Incorrect posting.
Unresolved reversals.
Outstanding clearing records.

3. Exception management
An exception is created when a reconciliation process identifies an unresolved difference.

The exception must include:
Exception identifier.
Payment identifier.
Amount under investigation.
Originating system.
Root cause.
Assigned employee.
Resolution status.
Supporting evidence.
