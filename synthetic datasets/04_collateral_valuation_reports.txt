1. Collateral overview
Collateral is an asset pledged to support a financial obligation.

Collateral may include:
Residential property.
Commercial property.
Land.
Fixed deposits.
Eligible securities.
Equipment.
Other accepted financial assets.

Collateral valuation helps determine the security available to support a loan.

3. Loan-to-value calculation

Loan-to-value ratio is calculated as:
LTV = Loan Amount / Collateral Value × 100
