Banking Operations and Financial Products
1. Bank Information
Apex National Bank (ANB) is a fictional commercial bank established in 2010. It provides retail banking, corporate banking, personal loans, and digital banking services.
The bank operates through its headquarters in Hyderabad, India.

2. Banking Products

Apex National Bank offers the following products:

2.1 Savings Account
The Savings Account is designed for individual customers.
    Minimum balance: INR 5,000
    Annual interest rate: 3.5%
    Account type: Deposit Account
    Eligible customers: Individual customers
    Personal Loan

2.2 Personal Loan
The Personal Loan is an unsecured credit facility offered to eligible customers.
    Minimum loan amount: INR 50,000
    Maximum loan amount: INR 2,000,000
    Annual interest rate: 10.5%
    Maximum repayment period: 60 months

2.3 Business Loan
The Business Loan is offered to registered businesses for operational and expansion requirements.
    Minimum loan amount: INR 500,000
    Maximum loan amount: INR 10,000,000
    Annual interest rate: 12%
    Maximum repayment period: 84 months

3. Banking Operations
    Apex National Bank maintains customer accounts and processes financial transactions.
    Customers can apply for loans through bank branches or digital banking channels.
    The bank evaluates loan applications using customer income, credit history, repayment capacity, and existing financial obligations.

4. Banking Relationships
    Apex National Bank provides Savings Accounts.
    Apex National Bank provides Personal Loans.
    Apex National Bank provides Business Loans.
    Customers maintain accounts with Apex National Bank.
    Customers apply for loans offered by Apex National Bank.
5. Technology Infrastructure

The bank uses a fictional Core Banking System called ApexCore to manage customer accounts, loan records, and financial transactions.
ApexCore integrates with the bank's digital banking platform.
