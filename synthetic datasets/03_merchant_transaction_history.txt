4. Merchant monitoring
Merchant monitoring evaluates:
Transaction frequency.
Average ticket size.
Refund frequency.
Chargeback ratio.
Settlement delays.
Unusual geographic patterns.
Merchant account status.

A high chargeback ratio may lead to enhanced monitoring and a merchant risk assessment.
