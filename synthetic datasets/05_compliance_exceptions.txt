1. Exception management
A ComplianceException represents a deviation from an applicable regulatory obligation, policy, standard, or internal control. Each exception is assigned a unique identifier and tracked through resolution.

3. Exception lifecycle
OPEN → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → PENDING_VALIDATION → CLOSED.

An exception cannot be marked CLOSED solely because a corrective action has been assigned. Closure requires evidence that the issue has been resolved and independently validated where required.
