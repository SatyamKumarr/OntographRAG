INFORMATION ASSET REGISTER AND CLASSIFICATION REPORT
Reporting Period: October 2026

1. Purpose
This report provides a structured inventory of information assets used across banking operations.
It identifies important information assets, their business ownership, classification, storage locations, security requirements, access responsibilities, and associated risks.

The register supports information security governance, data protection, business continuity, vulnerability management, and regulatory compliance.

3. Detailed asset observations
3.1 Customer information database
Asset AST-1001 stores customer identification information, contact details, account references, and customer-related operational records.

The asset supports customer onboarding, account servicing, compliance verification, and banking operations.

Security requirements:
Role-based access control.
Encryption of sensitive information.
Periodic access reviews.
Database activity monitoring.
Retention and disposal controls.

Associated risks: Unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, data corruption, and inappropriate retention.

3.2 Core banking application
Asset AST-1002 supports account servicing, financial transactions, balance maintenance, and banking operations.
Due to its critical operational importance, the application requires strict access governance and availability monitoring.

Security requirements:
Privileged access management.
Change management.
Transaction audit logging.
Application security testing.
Backup and recovery validation.

Associated risks: Service interruption, unauthorized modification, configuration weaknesses, and privileged access misuse.

3.3 Digital banking platform
Asset AST-1003 provides customers with digital access to banking services.

The platform processes authentication requests, account inquiries, and customer banking activities.

Security requirements:
Secure authentication.
Session management.
Application vulnerability monitoring.
Access logging.
Customer data protection.

3.4 Payment processing database
Asset AST-1004 maintains payment processing information and transaction-related operational records.
Its security classification is restricted because unauthorized access or modification could affect financial operations.

Security requirements:

Restricted database access.
Transaction integrity controls.
Audit trail preservation.
Backup validation.
Segregation of administrative responsibilities.

3.5 Customer consent repository
Asset AST-1008 stores records of customer consent, consent status, collection dates, processing purposes, and withdrawal requests.

The repository supports privacy governance and evidence of customer authorization.

Security requirements:
Consent record integrity.
Access restriction.
Version history.
Retention management.
Traceability of consent changes.

6. Asset lifecycle management
Information assets must be managed throughout their lifecycle.
Acquisition and creation

New information assets must be registered, assigned an owner, and classified according to business sensitivity.
Usage and maintenance
Access permissions, security configurations, and ownership details must be maintained.
Periodic review
Asset owners must validate asset classification, business purpose, and access requirements.
Retention and disposal
Assets must be retained according to applicable legal, regulatory, and organizational requirements.
Disposal activities must ensure that sensitive information is securely removed or destroyed.

7. Management recommendations
Maintain a centralized information asset register.
Assign accountable business owners.
Classify information based on sensitivity.
Review access permissions periodically.
Link assets with vulnerabilities and security incidents.
Document asset lifecycle changes.
Validate backup coverage for critical assets.
Review third-party information asset dependencies.
Ensure privacy requirements are reflected in asset controls.
