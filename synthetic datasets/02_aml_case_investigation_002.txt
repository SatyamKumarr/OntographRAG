2. Background
The Transaction Monitoring System identified unusual activity in an account that had remained substantially inactive for approximately 18 months. Before the alert, the account showed limited activity, primarily consisting of occasional small deposits and periodic maintenance charges.

On 05 August 2026, the account received a high-value credit followed by multiple transfers to external beneficiaries. The change in account behavior triggered an automated dormant-account monitoring alert.

6. Risk Indicators
The investigation identified the following indicators:
Reactivation following an extended period of inactivity.
High-value credit immediately after reactivation.
Multiple outward transfers.
Beneficiaries not previously observed in the account history.
Transaction volume inconsistent with the customer's previously documented activity.
Insufficient initial explanation of the economic purpose.

The indicators justified enhanced due diligence but did not establish criminal activity.

7. Investigation Activities
The investigator initiated:
Review of historical account activity.
Verification of the source of the INR 1,800,000 credit.
Review of beneficiary identities.
Confirmation of customer contact details.
Review of recent KYC information.
Assessment of whether account access changes occurred before reactivation.

The team also requested supporting documentation for the significant credit.

9. Investigation Findings

The account exhibited unusual reactivation behavior.

The monitoring system correctly identified a material deviation from historical activity.

At the time of the report, the source of the significant credit and the economic purpose of the subsequent transfers remained subject to verification.

The case was escalated to enhanced review.

10. Corrective Actions
