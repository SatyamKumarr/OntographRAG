DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY COMPLIANCE REPORT
Reporting Period: October 2026

1. Purpose
This report provides an overview of customer data protection activities, privacy controls, consent management, personal information processing, data retention, and privacy compliance monitoring. It evaluates how personal and financial information is collected, processed, stored, accessed, retained, and protected across banking operations.

The report connects data protection with information security, customer consent, regulatory obligations, privacy processing activities, and compliance assessments.

3. Detailed data protection observations
3.1 Customer onboarding information
Activity DPA-1001 involves the collection and processing of customer identity information during account opening and onboarding. The information supports customer verification and applicable regulatory requirements. The onboarding process must ensure that personal information is collected for documented purposes and accessed only by authorized personnel.

Required controls:
Purpose limitation.
Data minimization.
Access restrictions.
Secure storage.
Retention management.
Status: COMPLIANT.

3.2 Account servicing information
Activity DPA-1002 involves the processing of financial and account-related information for routine banking services. Access must be restricted to authorized personnel and systems. Data integrity and confidentiality must be maintained throughout the processing lifecycle.
Status: COMPLIANT.

3.3 Marketing communication consent
Activity DPA-1003 involves the use of customer contact information for marketing communications. The privacy review identified the need to validate consent records and ensure that customer preferences are accurately reflected in communication systems. The organization must ensure that withdrawal requests are appropriately propagated to relevant communication channels.

Required actions:
Validate consent records.
Review communication preferences.
Identify withdrawn consent.
Synchronize consent status.
Maintain evidence of consent changes.
Status: REVIEW_REQUIRED.

3.4 Transaction monitoring information
Activity DPA-1004 involves the processing of financial transaction information for monitoring and applicable regulatory obligations. The processing must be supported by documented purposes, appropriate access controls, and applicable retention requirements.
Status: COMPLIANT.

3.5 Customer analytics
Activity DPA-1007 relates to the use of customer behavioral information for analytical activities. The privacy function must review the purpose, data categories, consent requirements where applicable, and retention arrangements. The activity remains subject to additional privacy validation.
Status: REVIEW_REQUIRED.

5. Privacy compliance monitoring
Privacy compliance monitoring focuses on ensuring that customer information is processed in accordance with documented organizational requirements and applicable regulations. 

The principal monitoring areas include:
Data collection: Ensure that personal information is collected for legitimate and documented purposes.
Data processing: Ensure that processing activities remain within their approved purposes.
Data access: Restrict personal information access to authorized employees and systems.
Data retention: Maintain documented retention periods and disposal requirements.
Consent management: Maintain traceable records of consent, preferences, and withdrawal requests.
Third-party processing: Review contractual and operational data protection arrangements.
