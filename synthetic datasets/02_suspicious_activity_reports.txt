Financial Crime Compliance Repository
1. Purpose
This document maintains the synthetic Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) register for Meridian National Bank.
The SAR repository supports investigation tracking, reporting decisions, compliance oversight, and regulatory evidence management.

A suspicious activity alert, an internal investigation, and a regulatory SAR filing represent different operational events.An automated monitoring alert identifies potentially unusual activity. An AML investigation examines the circumstances and supporting evidence.

An authorized compliance decision determines whether reporting obligations are triggered.
A SAR record should therefore distinguish between:

Alert generated.
Investigation initiated.
Investigation completed.
Reporting decision pending.
Reporting decision approved.
Regulatory report submitted.
Follow-up or supplementary reporting required.

5. SAR Lifecycle Management

The synthetic institution follows the following internal reporting lifecycle:

Transaction Monitoring generates an alert.
Financial Crime Compliance creates an AMLCase.
The investigator identifies related customers, accounts, transactions, and beneficiaries.
Evidence artifacts are attached to the case.
The investigator prepares an investigation assessment.
Compliance personnel review the findings.
An authorized reporting decision is recorded.
Where required, a regulatory report is submitted.
Submission evidence and reference details are retained.
The institution conducts ongoing monitoring and any required follow-up.

SAR confidentiality must be preserved. Access to SAR-related information should be restricted to authorized personnel.
