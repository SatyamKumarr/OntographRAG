1. Operational risk overview
Operational risk is the risk of loss arising from inadequate or failed internal processes, people, systems, or external events.

The bank evaluates operational risks across:
Payment operations.
Customer onboarding.
AML operations.
Loan processing.
Reconciliation.
Information technology.
Third-party services.
Business continuity.
Data management.

2. Risk assessment RA-1001: Payment Processing
Risk ID: RISK-1001
Assessment ID: RA-1001
Domain: Payment Processing
Likelihood: 3
Impact: 4
Inherent Score: 12
Classification: HIGH
Owner: Operations Risk
Description

A payment processing outage could delay customer transactions and settlement activities.

Existing controls
Transaction monitoring.
Processing availability monitoring.
Exception handling.
Operational incident escalation.
Reconciliation controls.
Recommended treatment
Strengthen transaction monitoring.
Improve service availability reporting.
Test recovery procedures.
Establish documented incident escalation.
