1. Loan origination overview
Loan origination is the process of receiving, evaluating, approving, and disbursing a credit request.

The process involves the Customer, Loan, CreditFacility, Account, Collateral, RiskAssessment, ComplianceAssessment, Employee, and Authorization entities.

2. Loan origination lifecycle
Customer submits application.
Application details are recorded.
KYC verification is completed.
Income and employment information is evaluated.
Credit bureau information is reviewed.
Repayment capacity is assessed.
Collateral is evaluated where applicable.
Credit risk is calculated.
Approval authority reviews the application.
Credit facility is created.
Loan agreement is executed.
Funds are disbursed.
Repayment monitoring begins.
