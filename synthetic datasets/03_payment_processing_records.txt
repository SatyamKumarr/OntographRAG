1. Overview
Payment processing is the operational function responsible for receiving, validating, authorizing, routing, executing, and recording financial payment instructions.

Meridian National Bank supports the following payment channels:

Account-to-account transfers
NEFT-style domestic transfers
RTGS-style high-value transfers
IMPS-style instant payments
UPI-style digital payments
Debit card transactions
Credit card transactions
Merchant acquiring transactions
Digital wallet payments
Internal account transfers

Each payment is represented by a unique Payment entity and may be associated with a PaymentInstruction, PaymentMessage, Authorization, FinancialTransaction, Account, Customer, Beneficiary, Merchant, ClearingRecord, Settlement, and Reconciliation.

2. Payment lifecycle

A standard payment follows these operational stages:
Payment initiation.
Customer authentication.
Payment instruction creation.
Account and beneficiary validation.
Authorization assessment.
Transaction routing.
Payment execution.
Clearing, where applicable.
Settlement.
Reconciliation.
Customer notification.
Transaction archival.

The payment may be rejected, reversed, refunded, disputed, or placed under investigation at different stages.
