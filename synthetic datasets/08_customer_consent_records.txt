3. Detailed consent observations
3.1 Marketing communication consent
Consent CNS-1001 authorizes the applicable marketing communication activity recorded for the customer.
The organization must ensure that the consent record is linked to the relevant communication systems.
The customer preference must be respected during marketing campaign execution.

Required controls:
Maintain the consent collection date.
Record the consent purpose.
Maintain evidence of authorization.
Provide an appropriate withdrawal mechanism.
Synchronize customer preferences.

Status: ACTIVE.

3.2 Transaction notification consent
Consent CNS-1002 relates to transaction notification services.The record supports communication preferences for account-related notifications. The organization should distinguish service-related communications from optional promotional communications.

Status: ACTIVE.

3.3 Withdrawn personalized offer consent

Consent CNS-1003 was withdrawn by the customer. The withdrawal requires appropriate processing across systems that rely on the consent record. The organization should verify that the withdrawn status has been reflected in applicable communication and personalization platforms.

Status: WITHDRAWN.

3.4 Electronic statement delivery
Consent CNS-1004 relates to electronic delivery of customer statements. The organization should maintain evidence of the customer's communication preference and ensure that statements are delivered through authorized channels.

Status: ACTIVE.

3.5 Customer analytics consent review
Consent CNS-1005 requires additional review to verify that the recorded authorization and associated processing purpose are appropriately documented. The privacy office must validate the applicable processing basis and consent evidence.

Status: PENDING_REVIEW.

3.6 Withdrawn promotional email consent
Consent CNS-1006 records withdrawal of promotional email communication authorization. The marketing communication system must reflect the withdrawal. Evidence of the preference update should be retained for compliance review.

5. Consent governance controls
Consent collection
Consent must be recorded with an identifiable customer reference, purpose, collection channel, and timestamp.

Consent evidence
The organization must maintain evidence demonstrating how and when the customer preference was recorded.

Consent withdrawal
Withdrawal requests must be registered and communicated to relevant processing systems.

Purpose limitation
Consent must not be interpreted as authorization for unrelated processing activities.

Periodic review
Consent records must be reviewed to identify outdated, incomplete, withdrawn, or inconsistent records.

7. Management recommendations
Maintain a centralized consent register.
Record consent purpose and collection channel.
Preserve consent evidence.
Process withdrawal requests promptly.
Synchronize consent status across systems.
Separate service notifications from promotional communications.
Review third-party consent requirements.
Monitor consent records requiring additional review.
Integrate consent management with privacy processing activities.
Conduct periodic consent governance assessments.
