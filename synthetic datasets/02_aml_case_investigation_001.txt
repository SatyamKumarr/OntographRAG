2. Background

The Financial Crime Monitoring System generated an alert after identifying unusual transaction activity associated with customer CUST-1001.

The customer maintained a personal savings account that historically demonstrated relatively stable transaction behavior. Typical account activity consisted of salary credits, utility payments, household expenses, and occasional domestic transfers.

During the monitoring period, the account received several incoming transfers from unrelated counterparties. A significant proportion of the received funds was transferred to multiple beneficiaries within a short period.

The transaction pattern differed materially from the customer's historical financial behavior.

The monitoring system identified three primary indicators:

Multiple incoming transfers from unrelated accounts.
Rapid onward movement of funds shortly after receipt.
Transaction values inconsistent with the customer's declared financial profile.

The alert was escalated to the Financial Crime Compliance Department for investigation.

9. Investigation Findings

The initial investigation established that the account experienced unusual transaction activity during the review period.

The customer had not provided sufficient supporting documentation to explain all incoming and outgoing transactions at the time of initial assessment.

The compliance team determined that enhanced due diligence was appropriate.

The investigation remained open pending:

Source-of-funds clarification.
Verification of beneficiary relationships.
Review of supporting invoices and agreements.
Assessment of whether the observed activity requires regulatory reporting.
10. Corrective and Preventive Actions

Action CA-AML-001: Perform enhanced due diligence.

Action CA-AML-002: Review customer transaction history over the preceding six months.

Action CA-AML-003: Obtain supporting documents for significant incoming transfers.

Action CA-AML-004: Review beneficiary relationships.

Action CA-AML-005: Maintain enhanced monitoring until the investigation is resolved.
