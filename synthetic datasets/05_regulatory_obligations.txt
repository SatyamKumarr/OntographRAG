1. Purpose
A RegulatoryObligation represents a requirement that a financial institution must satisfy under an applicable law, regulation, direction, or regulatory framework.

The institution maintains an obligation register linking regulatory requirements to policies, controls, assessments, evidence, and reports.

2. Synthetic obligations
OBL-1001: Customer Due Diligence
Domain: KYC
Reference: Applicable RBI KYC requirements
Requirement: Maintain appropriate customer identification and due diligence records.
Responsible Function: Customer Compliance
Evidence: KYC profiles and verification records
Status: ACTIVE
OBL-1002: Suspicious Transaction Reporting
Domain: AML
Reference: Applicable PMLA and reporting requirements
Requirement: Evaluate and report suspicious activity through appropriate processes.
Responsible Function: AML Operations
Evidence: AML investigations and SAR records
Status: ACTIVE
OBL-1003: Information Security Governance
Domain: Information Security
Reference: Applicable RBI security requirements and internal standards
Requirement: Maintain information security controls and incident management.
Responsible Function: Information Security
Evidence: Security assessments and incident reports
Status: ACTIVE
OBL-1004: Personal Data Protection
Domain: Privacy
Reference: Applicable data protection requirements
Requirement: Process personal information according to applicable legal obligations and documented purposes.
Responsible Function: Privacy Office
Evidence: Consent records and processing activity registers
Status: ACTIVE
