1. Purpose

This document describes the operational processes governing account opening, account maintenance, account servicing, and account closure.

An Account represents a financial relationship maintained by a customer with the bank.

Account opening is subject to customer identification, KYC verification, product eligibility, authorization, and applicable compliance controls.

The Account ontology class is a subclass of FinancialProduct in the supplied ontology.

Operational account ownership must be represented separately from the IS_A ontology relationship.

2. Account Opening Lifecycle

The account opening process consists of the following stages.

Stage 1: Customer Registration

A customer is registered in the Customer Master System.

A unique Customer ID is assigned.

The registration process captures the minimum information required for identification, customer classification, and servicing.

Stage 2: KYC Verification

The customer's identity and supporting documents are reviewed.

The KYC team records the verification outcome.

Accounts requiring additional due diligence must follow the applicable compliance approval process.

Stage 3: Product Selection

The customer selects an eligible financial product.

Examples include:

Savings Account.
Current Account.
Premium Savings Account.
Corporate Current Account.
Digital Wallet.
Investment Settlement Account.
Stage 4: Account Creation

The Core Banking System generates a unique Account ID.

The account record is linked to the customer and selected product.

Stage 5: Authorization

Account creation is approved by an authorized employee or automated approval workflow.

The authorization record includes the responsible role, approval status, timestamp, and associated account reference.

Stage 6: Activation

Following successful approval, the account is activated.

The account becomes available for permitted banking transactions.

11. Account Maintenance Procedures

Account maintenance includes changes to customer details, communication preferences, authorized representatives, transaction limits, product settings, and account status.

All maintenance activities must be attributable to an authorized user or approved system process.

Customer Information Changes

Customer address and contact information updates must be validated and recorded.

Material changes to customer identity or business information may trigger a KYC refresh.

Account Status Changes

An account may have the following operational statuses:

Pending: Account creation is incomplete.
Active: Account is available for permitted transactions.
Dormant: Account meets internal inactivity criteria.
Restricted: Certain operations are limited under authorized controls.
Suspended: Account activity is temporarily stopped.
Closed: The account relationship has ended.

These statuses describe operational state. They are not ontology classes in the supplied ontology.

Account Closure

Account closure requires:

Customer request or authorized business reason.
Identity and authority verification.
Review of pending transactions.
Settlement of outstanding obligations.
Approval by authorized personnel.
Final account status update.
Record retention according to applicable requirements.
