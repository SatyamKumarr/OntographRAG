1. Assessment overview
ComplianceAssessment represents a formal evaluation of whether an organizational process or business activity satisfies applicable obligations and internal requirements.

An assessment records:
Assessment identifier.
Scope.
Assessment period.
Applicable standard.
Responsible compliance entity.
Findings.
Evidence.
Exceptions.
Overall status.
