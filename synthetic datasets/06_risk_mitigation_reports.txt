4. Residual risk

Residual risk represents exposure remaining after existing controls and treatment activities are considered.

Residual risk should be reassessed after the implementation of treatment actions. It must not automatically be assumed to be lower than inherent risk without supporting evidence.

5. Management recommendations
Prioritize high-risk open treatments.
Require evidence before treatment closure.
Monitor overdue corrective actions.
Review residual risk periodically.
Escalate material changes to senior management.
Link risk reporting with internal audit findings.
