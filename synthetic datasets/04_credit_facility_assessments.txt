1. Credit facility overview
A CreditFacility defines the approved credit arrangement between the financial institution and the borrower.

A facility may include:
Approved credit limit.
Utilized amount.
Available amount.
Interest rate.
Maturity date.
Repayment conditions.
Security requirements.
Covenants.
Review frequency.

Loan is a specialized FinancialProduct and CreditFacility in the supplied ontology.

3. Credit assessment dimensions

The assessment evaluates:

Repayment capacity.
Debt-to-income ratio.
Existing liabilities.
Income stability.
Business cash flow.
Collateral coverage.
Industry risk.
Customer repayment behavior.
