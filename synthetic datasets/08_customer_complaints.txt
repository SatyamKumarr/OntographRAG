CUSTOMER COMPLAINT MANAGEMENT REPORT
Reporting Period: October 2026

1. Purpose
This report provides a consolidated overview of customer complaints received through banking branches, digital banking channels, telephone support, email, and other customer service channels. It documents complaint categories, customer concerns, associated financial products, complaint severity, investigation findings, resolution ownership, and escalation status.

The report supports customer service quality monitoring, regulatory compliance, operational risk management, and continuous improvement.

3. Detailed complaint observations
3.1 Unauthorized transaction concern
Complaint CMP-1001 was registered after a customer reported a transaction that was not recognized. The customer service team referred the matter to the payment investigation function for verification.  The investigation requires review of transaction records, authentication history, and applicable customer authorization evidence.

Actions initiated:
Complaint registered.
Customer acknowledgment issued.
Transaction records requested.
Payment investigation initiated.
Case escalated to the relevant operational team.
Current status: INVESTIGATING.

The complaint must not be considered a confirmed fraud incident until the investigation establishes the relevant facts.

3.2 Delayed refund
Complaint CMP-1002 relates to a refund that had not been reflected in the customer's account within the expected service period. The customer service team initiated a review of the payment and refund processing records. The payment operations team is responsible for confirming the transaction status.
Current status: IN_PROGRESS.

3.3 Incorrect loan installment debit
Complaint CMP-1003 concerns a customer dispute regarding an installment debit associated with a loan account.
The complaint was escalated to credit operations for review of the repayment schedule and transaction records.
The investigation must determine whether the debit was consistent with the approved loan repayment arrangement.
Current status: OPEN.

3.4 Account access difficulty
Complaint CMP-1004 relates to difficulty accessing a savings account through the digital banking channel.The customer support team reviewed the access issue and coordinated with digital banking support. The customer was informed after the issue was addressed.

Current status: RESOLVED.

3.5 Delayed payment processing
Complaint CMP-1005 concerns a payment that was not reflected within the expected processing period. Payment operations is reviewing the transaction processing status and settlement records. Current status: IN_PROGRESS.

3.6 Incorrect account charges
Complaint CMP-1006 involves a customer concern regarding charges applied to a savings account. The branch operations team must verify the applicable account tariff, transaction history, and charge authorization.
Current status: OPEN.

6. Complaint resolution performance
Complaint resolution should be evaluated using documented service-level targets, actual resolution time, customer communication, and evidence of corrective action.

The organization should distinguish between:
Complaints received.
Complaints acknowledged.
Complaints under investigation.
Complaints resolved.
Complaints reopened.
Complaints escalated to management.

Resolution should be recorded only after the underlying customer concern has been addressed and appropriate evidence is available.

7. Management recommendations
Prioritize high-severity customer complaints.
Monitor unresolved complaint aging.
Establish clear complaint ownership.
Maintain communication with affected customers.
Review recurring complaint categories.
Link complaints with operational incidents.
Validate refunds and disputed transactions.
Monitor complaint resolution service levels.
Conduct root cause analysis for recurring issues.
Escalate material complaints to management.
