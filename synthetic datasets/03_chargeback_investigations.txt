1. Chargeback overview
A chargeback is a transaction dispute initiated through a card payment dispute process.

Typical reasons include:
Customer does not recognize the transaction.
Goods or services were not received.
Duplicate transaction.
Incorrect transaction amount.
Processing error.
Merchant-related dispute.

A Chargeback is represented as an ontology class under FinancialTransaction.

3. Chargeback lifecycle
Customer raises dispute.
Bank creates chargeback record.
Original payment is identified.
Merchant evidence is requested.
Evidence is reviewed.
Dispute decision is recorded.
Financial adjustment is processed where applicable.
Case is closed.
